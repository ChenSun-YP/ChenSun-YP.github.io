About

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Southern California, advised by Dong Song in the Neural Modeling and Interface Lab, where I study learning and synaptic plasticity from neural recordings. My work includes inferring plasticity rules from endogenous spikes, modeling neural time series, and investigating the effects of miniaturized transcranial magnetic stimulation with electromagnetic simulations. I use Python and PyTorch for modeling, signal processing, and neural decoding.

I previously studied Computer Science at Queen’s University and worked on deep spiking neural networks in its Machine Intelligence & Biocomputing Laboratory. My industry experience includes full-stack development at ByteDance and recruitment recommendation systems at Mesoor AI.

Selected Projects

  • LitAgent

    An agentic literature assistant that ingests Zotero libraries, indexes papers, and answers questions with citations. Includes interactive wiki and relation-map interfaces for exploring connections across papers.

  • GazeReader

    A spatial PDF reader for Apple Vision Pro, built with Swift, RealityKit, and ARKit. Uses eye tracking and hand gestures to highlight read passages in an immersive environment.

  • EEG Foundation Challenge

    A PyTorch model for predicting behavioral performance from EEG across tasks and subjects, using subject- and session-specific embeddings and neural masking. Ranked in the top 10% of participating teams in 2025.

  • Thalamic Model for Task Segmentation

    A PyTorch model with self-generated task representations, trained in a custom OpenAI Gym environment to study task-boundary inference, lifelong learning, and adaptation without catastrophic forgetting.

  • EvoMedley

    An interactive melody generator that combines MIDI fragments from user-selected tracks using evolutionary algorithms and user-guided fitness. Explore the demo.
